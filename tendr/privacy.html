Privacy Policy

Tendr: Self-Massage

Last updated: 16/11/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slonapps (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the mobile application Tendr (the “App”).

Summary

  • The App is currently free. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads.
  • The App does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your onboarding answers, plan, session history, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The App does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, or Apple Health.
  • We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to collect basic usage statistics and crash reports. These never include your onboarding answers or health-related information.
  • We do not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Tendr is not a medical device and is not a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist, or other qualified healthcare professional. The App provides general self-massage and relaxation guidance only. See our Terms of Use.

Information We Process

Information You Provide (Stored On Your Device)

During onboarding and while using the App, you may answer questions about what is bothering you and how you live, such as your main goal, body zones you want to work on (for example neck, back, head, arms, legs, feet), your tension level, common complaints (for example a stiff neck, headaches, lower back discomfort, tight shoulders, tired legs, jaw tension, cold hands), hours spent at a desk, when tension appears, preferred time of day, sleep quality, stress level, activity level, previous massage experience, tools you have (hands, ball, roller, gua sha, massage gun), preferred session length, gender, and age.

Some of these answers may be considered health-related information. We treat them accordingly: they are used only inside the App to build and adjust your self-massage plan, they are stored only on your device, and they are never transmitted to us, to Google, or to anyone else. You can skip questions you prefer not to answer.

Please do not enter passwords, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other credentials. The App does not need them.

Activity and Progress Data

The App records which sessions and exercises you complete, when you did them, your streaks, and related settings (such as music and voice guidance preferences), so it can show your progress over time. This data is stored on your device. We do not hold a copy.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited, to understand how the App is used and to find and fix crashes.

  • What is collected: a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase (not linked to your name or Apple ID), device model, operating system version, app version, language, device country and App Store country, approximate region derived from your IP address, screens opened, and in-app events such as first launch, a session being started, an exercise being skipped, a session being quit or completed, repeat and share taps, and music or voice guidance being switched on or off. Events include the identifier of the routine (for example a workout id), elapsed time, and the number of completed exercises.
  • Crash reports: stack traces, device state at the time of the crash, and app diagnostics. In rare cases, the App records a non-fatal error with a short technical message.
  • What is never collected: your onboarding answers, complaints, sleep or stress levels, age, gender, or any other health-related information. These never leave your device.

Firebase does not have access to the IDFA in the App, and we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Google processes this data under its own terms: Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Audio and Media

The App plays instructional video, background music, and spoken guidance generated on your device using Apple’s built-in speech synthesis. The App does not record audio and does not access your microphone.

Support Correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to you and to improve the App.

Purchases, Subscriptions, and Advertising

The App is currently free. It contains no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no third-party advertising. If we introduce paid features or other services in the future, we will update this Policy and the App before they take effect.

How We Use Information

  • To operate App features such as your self-massage plan, guided sessions, and progress tracking (on your device).
  • To personalize your plan and recommendations based on what you provide (on your device).
  • To understand which features are used and to improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
  • To respond to support requests.

Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

  • Contract: providing the App and its features.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reporting, App stability, and basic usage analytics that do not identify you.
  • Consent: where required by local law for analytics. You can object to analytics at any time by contacting us.

Health-related information you enter in the App is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to us, so we do not process it as a data controller.

Data Sharing

We share limited technical information only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) — usage analytics and crash reporting, as described in Analytics and Crash Reporting.
  • Apple — App Store distribution and the App’s system services.

We do not share your onboarding answers, health-related information, or session history with anyone, because they do not leave your device.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Data Retention

Your plan, onboarding answers, session history, and settings remain stored on your device until you reset your data in the App or delete the App. Analytics and crash data may be retained by Google for the periods described in its documentation (Firebase Analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months by default) and by us for as long as necessary for the purposes described above. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

Security

Keeping your personal and health-related information on your device is our main protection: it never travels over a network. We also take reasonable measures to protect the limited technical information we do process. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your device and passcode secure.

Your Choices

  • Your data: You can reset your progress and plan in the App. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data.
  • Onboarding answers: You can skip questions you prefer not to answer, and you can redo onboarding to change your answers.
  • Analytics: The App has no separate analytics switch yet. If you do not want usage statistics collected, contact us and we will help, or stop using the App.
  • Music and voice guidance: You can turn them on or off during a session.

Data Deletion

Data stored on your device (plan, onboarding answers, session history, settings) is deleted when you reset your data in the App or delete the App. We cannot delete it for you because we never receive it.

To request deletion of analytics or crash data held by our service provider, email slonapps@gmail.com with the subject “Tendr data deletion”. If possible, include the approximate date you installed the App and your device model, so we can locate the related records. We will process your request within 30 days.

Your Rights (EEA/UK and similar regions)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Note that data kept only on your device is under your direct control and can be deleted by you at any time. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Users

Our service providers may process the information described above in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.
