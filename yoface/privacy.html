Privacy Policy

Yoface: Face Yoga

Last updated: 09/14/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slonapps (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the mobile application Yoface (the “App”).

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Face scanning runs entirely on your device. Camera images are processed locally and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not store your photos or video. Camera frames are analyzed in memory and discarded; only the resulting measurements stay on your device.
  • We do not use face scanning to identify you, and we do not create a faceprint for identification.
  • Your exercise history, progress, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We use third-party analytics and crash reporting to improve stability and performance. These never include your photos or scan images.
  • The App may offer subscriptions in the future; payments would be handled by Apple.

Yoface is not a medical device and is not a substitute for a doctor, dermatologist, or cosmetologist. The App provides general wellness and exercise content only. See our Terms of Use.

Information We Process

Camera and Face Scanning (On-Device Only)

The App can use your camera to analyze your face and suggest exercises or show changes over time. This analysis is performed locally on your device, using the device’s own processing capabilities and Apple’s on-device frameworks.

  • Camera images and video frames are processed in memory and then discarded.
  • Photos or video from the scan are not saved to your photo library, not saved by the App, and not uploaded to our servers or to any third party.
  • Only the derived result of the analysis — such as numeric measurements, zones, scores, and recommendations — is stored on your device so you can see your progress.
  • We do not receive these results, and we cannot see them.
  • The analysis is used for exercise guidance only. It is not used to recognize or identify you, and we do not build a biometric template for identification purposes.

Camera access is requested only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings. Without camera access, other parts of the App remain available.

Information You Provide

During onboarding and while using the App, you may provide information such as your goals, focus areas, age range, experience level, reminder preferences, and notes. This information is used to build and adjust your exercise plan inside the App.

Please do not enter passwords, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other credentials. The App does not need them.

Activity and Progress Data

The App records which exercises and sessions you complete, your streaks and regularity, and related settings, so it can show your progress over time. This data is stored on your device.

Device and App Data

We use third-party analytics and crash reporting services to process basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, region, approximate usage of screens and features, and crash logs. This helps us diagnose issues and improve the App.

This data does not include your photos, video, or camera frames. Where required, we ask for your permission before any tracking that relies on the device advertising identifier.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may send reminders about your practice. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings or in the App.

Purchases and Subscriptions (if applicable)

The App may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions now or in the future. Any such transactions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We do not receive your full payment details (such as credit card numbers). Apple may provide us with limited information such as purchase or subscription status so that we can unlock the corresponding features.

How We Use Information

  • To operate App features such as face analysis, exercise plans, tracking, and progress.
  • To personalize your program and recommendations based on what you provide.
  • To send reminders where you have enabled notifications.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To validate purchases and subscription status, where applicable.
  • To respond to support requests.

On-Device Analysis and Its Limits

Scan results and recommendations are generated automatically and are estimates only. They depend on lighting, camera angle, distance, and other conditions, and they may be inaccurate or inconsistent between sessions. They are general wellness information — not a diagnosis, medical assessment, or evaluation of your skin or health. Consult a qualified professional about any health or skin concern.

Data Sharing

We may share limited technical information with service providers that help us operate the App — for example, analytics and crash reporting. These providers are permitted to process data only for providing services to us.

We do not share your camera images, scan images, or scan results with anyone, because they do not leave your device.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

Data Retention

Camera frames used for scanning are not retained at all. Your plan, activity history, scan results, and settings remain stored on your device until you delete them in the App or delete the App. Crash and analytics data may be retained by our service providers for a limited period to investigate and prevent issues.

Security

Keeping face analysis on the device is our main protection: the images never travel over a network. We also take reasonable measures to protect the limited technical information we do process. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your device and passcode secure.

Your Choices

  • Camera: You can grant or revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Your data: You can reset your progress and delete your entries in the App, and deleting the App removes locally stored data.
  • Notifications: You can enable or disable reminders in iOS Settings or in the App.
  • Analytics: You can limit tracking in iOS privacy settings where supported.
  • Subscriptions: Where applicable, you can manage or cancel them in your Apple ID account settings.

Your Rights (EEA/UK and similar regions)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Note that data kept only on your device is under your direct control and can be deleted by you at any time. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Users

If you use the App from outside your country, the limited technical information described above may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.
